How BISure Works

Strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) designed to give you verifiable ground truth, not probabilistic hallucinations.

01

1. Ask a question

Ask any natural language query on BIS standards, mandatory QCOs, hallmarking rules, or certification procedures.

02

2. Retrieve verified BIS sources

ChromaDB semantic search retrieves exact chunks from official Gazette notifications, QCO orders, and BIS documentation.

3. Grounded answer with citations

Synthesized response directly cites official reference standards and clauses. If out of scope, the assistant explicitly declines to guess.
