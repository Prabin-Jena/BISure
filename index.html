The Verification Dilemma

Answers are easy to find. Proof is harder.

When asking regulatory questions, generic conversational models assemble fluent prose by blending official Gazette notifications with outdated forum posts, e-commerce hearsay, and expired proposals.

User Inquiry

"Are imported plastic toys required to carry the ISI mark before sale?"

Gazette Notification S.O. 853(E) Statutory Authority

"Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020: Compulsory ISI mark required under Scheme-I for all toys imported or manufactured in India."

Export-Import Blog (2018) Obsolete Context

"Claims toys only require self-declaration and sampling at customs ports without mandatory factory licensing."

Trade Forum Discussion (2022) Conflicting / Rumor

"Suggests that imported electronic toys fall under CRS Scheme-II, confusing IT hardware rules with the Toys QCO."

Ministry Draft Discussion Note Non-Binding

"Proposes future exemptions for micro-scale artisans that were not ratified in the final enforceable statutory order."

The Grounding Rule: Without deterministic source-mapping, plausible-sounding answers obscure whether a requirement is enforceable statutory law or obsolete speculation.

Evidence Connection

Connecting Claims to Statutory Proof

Before an answer is synthesized, every required claim is bound directly to supporting clauses in the retrieved documents. Each branch below represents an evidentiary connection.

Verified Regulatory Claim

"Gold jewellery sold by registered jewelers in mandatory districts must carry a Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) code alongside the BIS logo and purity grade."

Branch 01 • Statutory Basis

Gazette Notification S.O. 1234(E)

Issued under Section 14 of the BIS Act, 2016. Mandates compulsory hallmarking of gold jewellery in notified districts across India.

Branch 02 • Technical Specification

IS 1417:2016 Specification

Clause 4.2 formally specifies the three mandatory stamp marks: the BIS triangular mark, the fineness/caratage grade, and the HUID code.

Branch 03 • Traceability Rule

BIS Directive HQ/HM/2023

Prohibits the sale of hallmarked gold without HUID and establishes the consumer verification protocol via the official BIS CARE registry.

Traceable Synthesis

Every Assertion Has an Address

Synthesized responses cite source documents directly through inline citation numbers [01][02]. Citations are matched on the server from retrieved chunks to prevent fabricated metadata.

Question: Does Scheme-I (ISI Mark) or Scheme-II (CRS) apply to mobile phone chargers?

Mobile phone adapters and chargers fall strictly under Scheme-II (Compulsory Registration Scheme / CRS) [01], not Scheme-I ISI factory marking [02].

Under Scheme-II (CRS), manufacturers are granted registration based on self-declaration of conformity backed by safety testing reports from BIS-recognized laboratories conforming to IS 13252 (Part 1):2010 [01]. Unlike Scheme-I, CRS registration does not require pre-certification factory audits or surprise plant inspections prior to approval [02].

Resolved Statutory Citations
[01] CRS Scheme Regulation: Electronics & IT Goods Order, Gazette S.O. 2357(E), Schedule Item 1 (Power Adapters).
[02] BIS Guidance Document: Comparative Guide on Scheme-I (Factory Certification) vs. Scheme-II (Registration).
Answers are grounded in curated statutory documents. Users should refer to official Gazette publications for formal legal proceedings.
Integrity & Boundaries

Refusing to Guess When Evidence Is Insufficient

When available evidence is insufficient, BISure should not present uncertainty as certainty. When a query references non-mandatory standards or falls outside the curated corpus, the assistant explicitly declines to guess.

Evidence Support Meter 22% • Insufficient Context
Candidate Match: 0.22 Minimum Acceptance Threshold: 0.35
Explicit Scope Boundary Question: "What is the mandatory ISI standard for handcrafted wooden dining chairs?"

Transparent Decline Behavior: No Quality Control Order currently mandates compulsory ISI certification or CRS registration for handcrafted wooden furniture under current BIS regulations.

"Under current statutory orders, handcrafted wooden furniture is not covered by a mandatory Quality Control Order (QCO). BISure focuses strictly on mandatory standards and will not guess. For voluntary standards or technical specifications, please consult bis.gov.in."
Architecture Guarantees

Principles of Grounded Intelligence

Built for regulatory verification where accuracy matters and unsubstantiated guesses carry legal risks.

01

Source-Grounded

Every regulatory statement is synthesized from numbered, retrieved chunks of Gazette Notifications, Quality Control Orders, or Indian Standards. Numbered inline citations map directly to retrieved source documents.

02

Transparent

We surface the document title, source category, and retrieved clause references alongside every answer. You can independently evaluate the underlying statutory text without placing blind faith in a black-box model.

03

Honest About Uncertainty

When an inquiry addresses voluntary standards, draft proposals, or matters outside the curated corpus, BISure explicitly declines to answer rather than fabricating plausible conjectures.
