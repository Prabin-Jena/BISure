Theme Smart Automation
Target Authority Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
Core Architecture Local RAG with ChromaDB & Gemini

What BISure Covers (In-Scope)

Actively Supported
  • Mandatory QCOs: Identification of products requiring compulsory ISI marking prior to sale.
  • Gold Hallmarking: Legal purity carats, 6-digit HUID verification, and jeweler compliance steps.
  • Scheme Disambiguation: Clear contrast between Scheme-I (ISI mark) and Scheme-II (CRS for electronics).
  • License Validity: 7-digit CML license number format, validity checks, and consumer verification guidance.
  • Citation Integrity: Every answer provides specific Gazette or standard document citations.

Out of Scope (Intentionally Deferred)

Future Roadmap
  • Paid Standard Downloads: Paid Indian Standard PDFs (e.g. IS 1417 full text) are commercial documents sold by BIS and protected by copyright.
  • Physical Barcode Scanning: Camera-based computer vision for retail package barcodes (requires dedicated mobile client).
  • Direct Legal Complaint Filing: Lodging formal statutory complaints against vendors (citizens are directed to the official BIS CARE portal).
  • Testing Lab Booking: Commercial scheduling and fee collection for testing laboratories.
Use Cases

Who BISure Is For

Tailored regulatory clarity for stakeholders navigating Indian Standards, mandatory certification, and product verification.

Consumers

Verify Products

Confirm authentic ISI marks, check 7-digit CML license validity, and understand gold hallmarking rules and 6-digit HUID purity standards before purchasing.

Manufacturers

Check Requirements

Review mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs), determine compulsory certification applicability, and clarify Scheme-I versus Scheme-II (CRS) compliance scope.

Retailers & Distributors

Validate Markings

Ensure stocked inventory carries valid mandatory certification markings and cross-check supplier CML details against official standard guidelines.
